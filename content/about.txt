[care]
title: What I care about
text: The ideas I keep coming back to, whatever the stack.
items:
  - Do it by hand once, then automate it | The first time I do it by hand so I understand every step. After that it goes into a script, a Terraform module or a pipeline job. Steps done by hand are where drift comes from.
  - Rollback should be cheaper than debugging | Small changes, canary releases and GitOps make a bad deploy a quick revert instead of an all-hands investigation. If I understand the problem I fix it forward. If I don’t, I roll back.
  - Monitoring ships with the service | Dashboards and alerts go out with the service, not in a ticket for later. An alert should mean someone has to act. If it doesn’t, it’s noise, and people start ignoring the ones that matter.
  - Security checks go in the pipeline | I would rather have a scan that fails the build than a report nobody opens. Secrets stay in Vault or a secret manager, access is limited to what is needed, and credentials are rotated on a schedule.
  - Idle resources cost money | Idle GPUs and forgotten instances are wasted spend. Autoscaling and scheduled shutdowns are part of running a platform properly.
  - Write things down | Runbooks, onboarding notes and root cause reviews. The next person on call, sometimes me, shouldn’t have to work out again what we already learned.
