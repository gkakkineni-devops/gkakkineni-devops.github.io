[care]
title: What I care about
text: The ideas I keep coming back to, whatever the stack.
items:
  - Do it by hand once, then automate it | The first time, I want to understand the steps. The second time, it becomes a script, a Terraform module or a pipeline job. Manual work is where drift and 2 a.m. surprises come from.
  - Rollback should be cheaper than debugging | Small changes, canary releases and GitOps mean a bad deploy is a quick revert, not an all-hands investigation. Fix forward when you understand the problem, roll back when you don’t.
  - If it isn’t monitored, it isn’t done | Dashboards and alerts ship with the service, not in a follow-up ticket. And an alert should mean someone needs to act. If it doesn’t, it’s noise and it trains people to ignore the real ones.
  - Security belongs in the pipeline | A scan that fails the build is worth more than a report nobody reads. Secrets live in Vault or a secret manager, access follows least privilege, and credentials rotate on a schedule.
  - Cost is a reliability problem too | Idle GPUs and forgotten instances are money that could fund real work. Autoscaling and scheduled shutdowns are part of running a healthy platform.
  - Write it down | Runbooks, onboarding notes and honest root cause reviews. The next person on call, sometimes me, should not have to rediscover what we already learned.
